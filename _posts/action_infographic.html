RESEARCH vs ACTION
When to Stop Thinking and Start Doing
🚨 RESEARCH TRAP SIGNALS
  • ⏰ Researching >3 hours for 1-hour task
  • 🔄 Restarting analysis after new info
  • 📚 Reading "just one more article"
  • 🤔 Waiting for "perfect" information
  • 💭 Discussing plans more than executing
  • 📊 Creating elaborate comparison matrices
✅ ACTION TRIGGERS
  • ✨ Can make 80% confident decision
  • ⚡ Action is small and reversible
  • 💡 Cost of delay > cost of imperfection
  • 🎯 "Good enough" solution exists
  • ⏱️ Research time budget exhausted
  • 🔥 Bias toward action beats perfect plan

⏰ RESEARCH TIME BUDGETS

Routine Tasks 30 minutes max
Medium Decisions 2-3 hours max
Major Choices 8 hours max
Life Changes 20 hours max
SUFFICIENT INFO + SPEED > PERFECT INFO + DELAY
Most decisions can be corrected easier than they can be perfected
"The best time to plant a tree was 20 years ago.
The second best time is now.
The worst time is after researching tree species for 6 months."
